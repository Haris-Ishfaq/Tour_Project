The best time to visit is from April to September when the weather is pleasant, roads are clear, and you can enjoy greenery and mountain views.

No special permit is required for Pakistani citizens. However, visitors from other countries must have valid travel permission from AJK authorities.

Rajpothi is a peaceful local village with no hotels or private guest houses. The nearest place for accommodation is Danna, where a government guest house is available for visitors. It offers beautiful views and basic facilities for tourists.
